To my sleepy baby ♡

FOR
JULIE

Good morning, my baby Julie. ♡

Happy blessed Sunday morning, my love! Good morning to the prettiest, sweetest, and sleepiest girl in my world. If I could be beside you right now, I would give you the warmest hug, kiss your forehead, hold you close, and tell you how much I love you.

Baby, I know you didn't get enough sleep, and I wish I could be there to take care of you. I would fix your blanket, tell you to close your eyes for a little longer, and let you rest peacefully. You don't need to rush your morning, okay? Please be gentle with yourself today.

Drink some water, eat something when you're ready, and get some extra rest if you can. You don't need to be energetic or cheerful every second for me to love you. Even my sleepy baby, with her low battery and sleepy eyes, is still my favorite person ever. HAHAHA. Your battery is low, baby! Please connect to your pillow and recharge immediately. 😭♡

I miss you so much, love. Sometimes I wish I could explain how much I miss you without having to type a whole essay, but my heart has apparently decided that short messages are not enough when it comes to you.

I miss talking to you, hearing from you, spending time with you, holding your hand, and being close to you. I miss those little moments that might seem ordinary to other people but feel special to me because they're moments I get to share with you.

Sometimes I will just be doing something completely unrelated, and suddenly my brain goes, "Wait a minute. I miss Julie." THANK YOU, BRAIN. I WAS TRYING TO DO SOMETHING. 😭 But honestly, I wouldn't trade these feelings for anything. You're someone my heart loves thinking about.

I love you, baby Julie. I love your sweetness, your personality, your little habits, the way you care, and the happiness you bring into my days. I love the way even the smallest moments with you can become some of my favorite memories.

I don't need expensive dates or perfect moments every single day. I just want more time with you. I want to laugh with you, learn more about you, listen to your stories, support you when things get difficult, celebrate your achievements, and remind you that you have someone cheering for you.

I want to keep learning how to love you properly, baby. I know love isn't only about saying "I love you." It's also about patience, understanding, respect, honesty, and showing up for each other. I want my actions to make you feel the love I keep trying to put into words.

You don't have to be perfect for me. You don't have to hide your tired days, your worries, your quiet moments, or the times when you don't feel your best. You can be yourself with me. I want you to feel comfortable telling me how you really feel, and I want to keep learning how to understand you better.

And because it's Sunday, I want to thank God for you. I'm grateful for the moments we've shared and the memories we still get to make. I pray that our relationship continues to grow with God at the center, and that we keep choosing kindness, patience, and understanding.

My prayer for you today is that God gives you the rest your body needs, peace for your mind, joy in your heart, and strength for whatever this new week brings. May He protect you wherever you go, guide your decisions, and remind you that His love never leaves you.

If anything is weighing on your heart, I hope you remember that you can take things one step at a time. You don't need to figure everything out in one day. Rest when you're tired. Pray when you need comfort. Smile when something makes you happy. And please remember to take care of yourself, because you matter so much.

Baby, I hope this little website makes you smile. I made it because I wanted to give you something sweet to wake up to, something that reminds you that someone is thinking about you and praying for you this Sunday morning.

If I could send my hugs through this screen, you would already have received approximately one million of them. And if you could hear everything my heart wants to tell you, you would probably say, "Baby, okay na, I know!" HAHAHA. But what can I do? I miss you. I love you. And I want you to know it. ♡

So, my sleepy baby, here's your reminder for today: you are precious, you are appreciated, you are prayed for, and you are loved. You deserve rest, happiness, gentle moments, and a heart full of peace.

Have a beautiful and blessed Sunday, my love. May God fill your day with goodness, protect you from harm, and give you plenty of reasons to smile. I hope you get some extra sleep and feel a little better as the day goes on.

And whenever you miss me, remember that somewhere, there's a person smiling at the thought of you, wishing he could hold your hand, and looking forward to the next time you get to be together.

I miss you so much, baby Julie. I love you more than this little webpage can explain, and I'm so happy that I get to care about someone as special as you. Here's to more prayers, more laughter, more memories, more hugs, and more beautiful days together. ♡

Always cheering for you,
your loving, slightly clingy baby ♡
